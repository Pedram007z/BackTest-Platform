## 1. What appears most, and what earns the most

| Content type | How common | What it earns |
|---|---|---|
| P&L / results reveals, "day in my life", live trades | Very common | Views and comments, few saves. Trust is falling; claims get challenged. |
| "I tested X" / myth-busting with data | Growing | **Saves and shares.** It answers a question people already have. Best fit for a backtesting brand. |
| Psychology and emotional stories (voice-over, character) | Common | Likes, comments, shares to friends ("send to someone who…"). Our e1 confirms this. |
| Explainers and lists (3 mistakes, risk math) | Common | Saves. Carousels beat reels on saves (2026 trackers). |
| Market moments (NFP, crash days, gold records) | Spiky | Timely reach. "What would you do?" drives comments. |
| Relatable pain and humour (stop hit, then reversal) | Common | **Highest shares** in finance. |

## 2. Visual styles that hold attention
- **A face on camera:** a person, or a character with a face, plus bold subtitles. Every big Persian page uses one.
- **The real chart:** a screen of a real chart with one big number on frame 1, in red or green.
- **Fast pacing:** a visual change every 1–1.5 s in the first 5 seconds, with a sound hit on each reveal.
- **Text placement:** the hook in the top third, where Instagram's UI doesn't cover it, and the same words spoken and shown.

## 3. Emotional triggers
- **Loss aversion and fear:** blowing up the account, a stop hit right before the reversal.
- **Surprise:** a counter-intuitive number, like a 61% win rate with a −59% account.
- **FOMO:** a move you missed, others getting ahead.
- **Desire:** years of experience in a weekend, more freedom.
- **Validation:** "it's not you, it's the sample size."
- **Controversy:** taking down a beloved indicator or rule.

## 4. Formats by goal
- **Watch to the end:** promise the answer in second 1, keep the open loop, and show the number at the end. Keep it under 25 s.
- **Saves:** rules, checklists and test-result tables.
- **Shares:** relatable pain plus a named target ("بفرستش برای رفیقی که…").
- **Comments:** A-or-B choices, "what would you do?", "guess the result", "which hour do you trade?".

## 5. Five content ideas Claude can make (all built from the platform's real data)
1. **«تستش کردم»:** one viral strategy per episode, tested on real data. Episode 1 is the MACD crossover on gold (numbers below).
2. **«اگه اون لحظه بودی»:** stop seconds before a real market moment, let viewers comment buy or sell, then reveal what happened (1 Aug 2025 NFP).
3. **«دادگاه افسانه‌ها»:** «زمان‌گرد» puts a trading myth on trial and gives a verdict in 20 s, with math or data.
4. **«درد تریدر»:** relatable pain played by the character, plus one practical fix. Made for shares.
5. **«یک عدد»:** one number that changes how you trade, such as losing-streak odds, expectancy or drawdown. Made for saves.

## 6. Hooks: structure, pacing, first 3 seconds
Most top trading reels open with one of six structures:
- **Contrarian claim:** «RSI رو کنار بذار».
- **Result first:** «۱۰ هزار دلار، ۳۰ روز، صفر».
- **Question with stakes:** «این معامله رو می‌گرفتی؟».
- **Direct call-out:** «اگه طلا ترید می‌کنی…».
- **Open loop:** «سومی حساب بیشتر تریدرها رو سوزونده».
- **Pattern interrupt:** a crash candle and a sound hit.

**Pacing:**
- The first word comes within 0.3 s.
- The hook is 8 words or fewer, about 2–2.5 s.
- The screen text is the spoken line.
- The first visual change comes by 1.5 s.
- The payoff is promised out loud ("آخرش عددش رو می‌بینی").

**First-3-second techniques:**
- a specific number or date;
- loss words such as ضرر, سوخت, صفر;
- eye contact plus head movement;
- a zoom-in on frame 1;
- withholding the answer;
- naming who it's for.

## 10. «زمان‌گرد», the character
A fantasy version of the founder's photo: a time-walking trader who rewinds any market day and trades it again.
- **Kept from the photo:** the thick swept-up dark hair with a loose fringe, the smoky-grey rectangular glasses with three silver dots at the hinges, the short dark beard and moustache, the calm dark eyes, and the patterned grey shirt (now a tunic).
- **Added:** a violet hooded cloak with gold candlestick embroidery, and an hourglass whose sand is candles.
