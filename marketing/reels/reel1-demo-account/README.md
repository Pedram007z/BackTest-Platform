# Reel 1: «تمرین با حساب دمو؟ وقت تلف کردنه‼»

| | |
|---|---|
| **Topic** | Practising on a demo account is slow and shows you only one kind of market. Backtesting shows five years in a weekend. |
| **Counter-belief** | Everyone says "practise on a demo account for a few months first". The reel says that's a waste of time. |
| **Length** | 58 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Persian, made to work without a voice-over. |
| **Outlier formats used** | The title box with ‼ in the first second; «داشبورد بک‌تست!!» (from the 3,198-like dashboard reveal); fill in the blank (from the 906-comment reel). |
| **Comment trigger** | «بدترین توصیه‌ای که به‌عنوان تریدر شنیدی: «اول ____»» / «جای خالی رو کامنت کن» |
| **Files** | `index.html` (self-contained page), `soundtrack.py` + `cues.json` (music), cover: `../covers/reel1-cover.jpg` |

**Numbers:**
- 100 trades at 1–2 setups per trading day is 50–100 trading days, shown as «≈ ۳ ماه».
- The dashboard figures are illustrative and labelled «داده‌ی نمونه».

## Soundtrack

`soundtrack.py`, with times from `cues.json`:
- A minor (Am–F–C–G), about 113 BPM. The bar length puts both drops (26.6 s and 43.6 s) on bar lines.
- Deep hits on the red ✕ and the «≈ ۳ ماه» stamp, and a whoosh on every cut.
- A calendar tick per day as it fills.
- A sudden drop-out on «ولی زمان، مشکلِ اصلی نیست», and a darker groove under "one kind of market".
- Risers and snare rolls into «۵ سال بازار رو» and «داشبورد بک‌تست!!».
- A bell when the trade hits its target, and a softer outro with a bell on the follow line.
