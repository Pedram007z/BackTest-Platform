# The method behind the reels

This document is the content strategy: the growth technique the reels follow, what the research on competing reels showed, and the formula every reel is built on. [PRODUCTION.md](PRODUCTION.md) covers how a reel is turned into a video.

---

## 1. The growth technique (from the account owner's brief)

**Step one: copy the outliers.**
- Follow 10 accounts in the niche: 5 of the top accounts and 5 small, newer ones (under about 10K followers).
- Study the top accounts video by video until you can say what makes each one distinctive.
- On the small accounts, look for the posts that got far more views than their usual (for example 50,000 views on an account that normally gets 4,000).
- Make something very similar to those outlier posts.

**Step two: the counter-belief hook.**
- Pick a topic with broad appeal.
- Open by contradicting something everyone has always been told, then connect it to what you actually want to say. Example: "Breakfast is the worst meal you can eat."
- The reference reel is `theiman_mkt`'s «زالو» reel (`DcjOF41RUlT`): "take other people's livelihood if you want to survive", which leads into how a small business survives against big ones.
- A message nobody watches does nothing, so attention comes first.

**Step three: the time-based "next hook" (suspense-based scriptwriting).**
- The first hook buys only about 3 seconds. If it works, viewers give you another 7–10 seconds.
- Before those run out, add a new hook (a question, a number, a reveal) or they scroll away. Hooks are needed throughout the video, not only at the start.
- Re-read or re-watch the script and tighten it wherever it starts to drag.
- Phrasing matters ("Script Type Two"):
  - Type One: "You've learned method A, but I also want to tell you about technique B." This is weak.
  - Type Two: "You've learned method A, but there's something **even more important** you haven't learned yet: technique B." This is better, because the viewer now wants to know what is more important and why.

Eight reels were watched frame by frame and their speech transcribed (automatically, so quotes are approximate). Likes and comments are as shown on Instagram on 11 Oct 2026.

### The counter-belief example (`theiman_mkt`, `DcjOF41RUlT`): 4,246 likes, 5,246 comments

- Opens on a taboo, then connects it to the real topic.
- Delays the answer: "two ways: the hard way… and the easy way".
- Mid-video text cards at about 28 s, 63 s and 84 s restart attention. These are the time-based next hooks.
- Ends with a keyword CTA: "comment «زالو» and I'll send it to you". **There are more comments than likes.**

### Patterns taken from the research

1. **First 1–2 seconds:** one huge line in a dark title box, with ‼, stating a claim.
2. **A new hook every 7–10 seconds:** a question, a number, "but there's something more important", "watch to the end".
3. **Show, don't tell:** dark trading dashboards, gauges, equity curves, real objects.
4. **A comment engine:** a blank to fill in, a side to pick (A or B), a number to type, or a keyword. Comments drive reach, and the biggest posts are the ones with the most comments.
5. **Captions on screen:** short lines, word by word, in a readable block. Most people watch without sound.

## 3. The reel formula used for all four reels

Every reel is about 52–58 seconds, 9:16, in Persian, and follows this skeleton. The times are typical; each reel's README has its exact timeline.

| Time | Beat | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.6 | **Counter-belief hook** | A line asks or sets up (top), then at about 1.1 s a red title box with ‼ contradicts what the viewer believes («…وقت تلف کردنه‼», «…و حسابت منفیه‼»). A visual proves the claim at the same moment, with a flash, a shake and an impact sound. |
| 3.6–9.5 | **The belief everyone repeats** | A chip «همه بهت گفتن:» / «همه فکر می‌کنن:» and a white speech bubble quoting the common advice. A ✓ flips to ؟. |
| ~7.5–9.5 | **Next hook (Type Two)** | «ولی یه عدد هست که هیچ‌کس بهت نمی‌گه…» or «…از خودِ استراتژی مهم‌تره…». It promises something more important. |
| 9.5–19 | **Proof with numbers** | A race, a counter or a dot chart that makes the point visible with real arithmetic. It ends on another Type Two line: «ولی این هنوز سخت‌ترین قسمتش نیست…» / «ولی ترسناک‌ترش اینه:». |
| 19–27 | **The bigger problem** | The deeper, scarier version: losing streaks, a collapse, five market regimes. It ends on a question hook («از کجا بدونی…؟», «پس ریسک درست چقدره؟»). |
| 27–35 | **The answer: backtesting** | «اسمش بک‌تسته» / «یک آخر هفته». A fast candle-by-candle replay (×60, years ticking by) shows it. |
| 35–44 | **The outlier format** | A dark-dashboard reveal («داشبورد بک‌تست!!», copying the 3,198-like reel), or a **save-worthy** card: a 4-rule checklist or a risk calculator, with a «این رو ذخیره کن» chip. |
| 44–end | **Pre-launch end card** | The logo, an amber «به‌زودی» badge, a slogan, «فالو کن تا روز لانچ رو از دست ندی» with a ringing bell, then a **comment trigger** card (fill in the blank, A or B, or a number to type) and a bouncing arrow. |

Hard cuts every 5–9 seconds get a small camera shake and a whoosh, and every reveal gets a sound.

## 4. Rules every reel follows

**Pre-launch (current status).** بک‌تست‌لب has not launched.
- **No website address** anywhere: not in the video, the cover or the caption.
- The end card shows «به‌زودی» and «فالو کن تا روز لانچ رو از دست ندی».
- Every caption ends with «بک‌تست‌لب به‌زودی میاد 🚀 / فالو کن تا روز لانچ رو از دست ندی 🔔» and the reel's comment question.
- After launch, the end card and captions can add the address.

**Honest numbers.**
- Every number shown is computed, never invented. `tools/verify_numbers.py` re-checks them all.
- Illustrative dashboards carry a «داده‌ی نمونه» (sample data) badge.
- A claim is never stronger than the maths allows. Example: in reel 4, 10% risk actually ends higher than 1% on the same trades, so the reel says "pick the risk whose worst day you can survive", not "1% makes the most".

**Language and look.**
- The language is conversational Persian («می‌گن», «درمیاره»).
- The brand look is the app's: dark background `#0d0b14`, violet accent, green for gain, red for loss, amber for warnings, and the Vazirmatn font.
- Each reel gets its own original soundtrack in a different key, so the reels don't sound like copies of each other and there is no copyright risk.

**Covers** (1080×1920, with the key content inside the 3:4 grid crop at y = 240–1680):
- the brand at the top
- the hook question
- a strong visual
- the red title box with ‼
- a violet chip teasing the answer

**Captions:**
- The first line repeats the hook with an emoji.
- Short lines follow, with the key numbers.
- A 📌 save-worthy block, if the reel has one.
- The pre-launch lines, then the comment question with 👇.
- Then `.` `.` `.` and about 10 hashtags.
- Write gains and losses as words («۴۶٪ سود», «۵۱٪ ضرر»), not as +/− signs, because Instagram's right-to-left layout moves the sign to the wrong side.

**Posting:**
- Post a few days apart: reel 1 → 2 → 3 → 4.
- Pin a first comment that answers your own question, so people have an example to copy:
  - «اول یه حساب دمو باز کن 😅»
  - «من B رو انتخاب می‌کنم؛ تو چی؟»
  - «من ۵ تا. تو چند تا؟»
  - «من روی ۱٪ ثابتم. تو چی؟»
- Reply to comments in the first hour.

**Voice-over (optional).** The reels are made to work silently.
- If a voice is added, lower the music 8–12 dB under it.
- Reel 1 has a timed Persian script in its README.
- ElevenLabs library voices (Kaveh fits the pace) need a paid plan to be used through the API.
