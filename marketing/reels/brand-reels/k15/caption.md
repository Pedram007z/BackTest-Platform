## The look

One night room: a brick wall of neon signs over a casino table, with no colour-block wipes. Each spoken word is a
neon tube that buzzes on when it is said. The whole line hangs there unlit first, so the hook is readable on the
first frame. Luck is the casino (a pink «شانس» sign, dice, chips, a shell game); strategy is a steady cyan sign.
Luck's tube sputters out on «شانس کم میاره», and the room blacks out on «تموم شد».
