## Facts on screen

- **The voice** is the user's take of dialog #1 («جواب: هیچ عددی») at its normal speed. Nothing is sped up and no
  pause is shortened. The voice is 39.0 s and the reel is 42.1 s.
- **«با وین‌ریت پنجاه درصد، توی صد تا معامله، به احتمال زیاد یه جا پنج تا ضرر پشت‌سرهم می‌خوری»** is plain
  probability. For a fair coin (a 50% win rate), the chance of at least one run of five or more losses somewhere in
  100 trades is about 81%.
- **The 10 × 10 grid is a simulation, and it's labelled «شبیه‌سازی نمونه».** It's 100 coin flips at a 50% win rate
  (seed 89, in `data.py`), with 49 wins and one five-loss streak. It isn't anyone's trading results.
- **No record is invented.** The counter for «رکورد ضرر پشت‌سرهم» spins and stops on «؟», because only a backtest
  gives that number. The ruler's line between «عادی» and «زنگ خطر» is also «؟».
- **The replay** is k13's real capture of the XAUUSD replay in BacktestLab, labelled «صفحه‌ی واقعی بک‌تست‌لب».
- **About «شروعش رایگانه»:** the free plan has one session, with the replay. Analytics (where the longest losing
  streak is shown) is on the paid plans. Don't call it free in a caption or a reply.
