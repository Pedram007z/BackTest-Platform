## Facts on screen

- **The voice** is k16's cut of the user's take (×1.15, 27.3 s); see `../k16/caption.md`.
- **The character is original.** He is drawn in flat vector shapes and is not anyone's likeness.
  - His mouth follows the voice (loudness → open, bright vs dark sounds → wide, from `../char/voicefeat.py`); it is an
    approximation, not phoneme-exact lip sync.
  - He blinks, nods on the stressed words, tilts his head on the questions, raises his brows on «می‌ترسی؟» and frowns
    on «کار نمی‌کنه».
  - His hand counts the three answers, points up at the screen and down at the bio, and he waves at the end.
- **No numbers are made up.** «۳» and «۵۰» are the script's own words. The balance, the cards, the knob, the lot bars
  and the bill are symbols.
- **The real screen** is k13's capture of the XAUUSD replay (14 April 2025), with the planned sell ringed on «تست».
- **About «شروعش رایگانه»:** the free plan has one session, with the replay; the reel shows only the replay.
