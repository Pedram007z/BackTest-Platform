## Facts on screen

- **Inspiration only.** The user shared a UI motion study as a reference. Only its principles were taken: one object on a
  light ground that keeps morphing into the next, a cursor driving it, and motion blur on fast moves. Every component,
  label and step of the story is BacktestLab's own. None of the reference's components, texts or sequence are copied.
- **The voice** is the user's take of dialog #2, the same take as k27 and k28, at its normal speed. Nothing is sped up.
  It's rendered at 60 fps.
- **The chart card** is a drawn sample, titled «چارت گذشته» and labelled «نمودار نمونه». It has no instrument and no price.
- **«استراتژی الف، ب، ج»** are placeholders for «هر استراتژی‌ای». They aren't real strategies or results.
- **The date picker** shows Farvardin 1404 correctly: the 1st was a Friday, and the 25th, a Monday, is the replay's real date.
- **The replay card** plays k13's real BacktestLab replay of XAUUSD, labelled «صفحه‌ی واقعی بک‌تست‌لب».
- **The week's counter** fills to twenty. «سودده؟» keeps loading with «تست کن تا بفهمی», so no result is claimed.
- **About «شروعش رایگانه»:** the free plan has one session, with the replay.
