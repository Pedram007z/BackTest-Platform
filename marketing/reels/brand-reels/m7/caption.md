# m7 · «شبیه‌ساز پرواز تریدرها»: caption

Facts behind it:
- The storm is Bitcoin's real crash of 5 August 2024 (15 Mordad 1403), shown as hourly candles from the platform's store (`storm.json`).
  - It opened the day at $58,161, touched $49,000 at 06:00 UTC and closed at $54,019.
- Platform claims are the ones already used in the launch caption:
  - real data from 2015;
  - 72 symbols across 5 markets;
  - the analytics (win rate, worst days, Monte Carlo).
- The analytics cards in the film are illustrations of those screens, not results: no numbers are shown.
